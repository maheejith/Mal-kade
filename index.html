Flower Care Guide (මල් සුරැකීමට)

Simple expert tips from Mal Kade to keep your Araliya, Roses, and Anthuriums blooming vibrantly for days.

🌼

Araliya Care

  • Float blooms in shallow water bowl with a sprinkle of water daily.
  • Keep away from direct sunlight to retain pristine white/yellow color.
🌹

Rosa Care

  • Trim stems diagonally by 1 inch under running water every 2 days.
  • Remove lower foliage below vase water level to prevent bacteria growth.
🌺

Anthurium Care

  • Mist heart-shaped spathes lightly with water to maintain glossy sheen.
  • Change vase water every 3 days; can stay fresh up to 2-3 weeks!
